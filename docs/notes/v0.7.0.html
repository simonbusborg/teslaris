Teslaris 0.7.0

November 10, 2026

  • Wake the car from the menu — an opt-in in Settings. With it on, Wake Car & Refresh appears while the car sleeps and fetches fresh numbers once it answers. Teslaris still never wakes the car by itself; waking needs the `vehicle_cmds` scope on your Tesla app and a new sign-in, and each wake counts as ten requests against your credits.
  • Errors that say what went wrong — when Tesla refuses a request, the menu now shows the status code and Tesla's own message, with a hint at the fix for the usual causes: an app not registered in your region, the wrong region, or a sign-in that has expired.
